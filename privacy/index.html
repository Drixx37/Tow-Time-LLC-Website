Overview

This notice explains what information our website collects, why it is used, and how to contact us with privacy questions.

Who We Are & Scope

Tow Time LLC (“Tow Time,” “we,” “us”) operates the website towtimellc.net. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you visit the website, contact our team, request towing or roadside assistance, ask about a private-property towing partnership, or submit a job application.

Information collected in person, over the telephone, or in connection with an actual tow or storage transaction may also be governed by applicable records and retention requirements. This policy concerns the website and related inquiries; it does not replace legally required towing or vehicle-storage notices.

Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Contact messages: name, email address, phone number (if provided), and message content.
  • Tow requests: name, phone number, optional email address, vehicle year/make/model/color, service requested, pickup and drop-off details, preferred date/time, and any notes you provide.
  • Property partnership inquiries: name, role, contact information, property/company name, property address and type, number of vehicle entrances, and parking challenges you choose to describe.
  • Employment applications: contact and mailing information, date of birth, references, requested position, availability, relevant experience, skills, answers to screening questions, and confirmation that the on-screen signature box was used.
  • Other communications: information you voluntarily share when communicating with us about your request.
Digital-signature detail: The online hiring form checks whether an applicant used the signature box and sends a signed-status indicator to the form processor. It does not send the drawn signature image.

Information collected automatically

Our website includes Google Analytics, which may collect information about pages visited, device/browser characteristics, approximate location derived from IP address, referral sources, and interactions through cookies or similar technologies. Website delivery and security providers may also process IP addresses and technical request data.

How We Use Information

  • Respond to inquiries (including property partnership requests), discuss possible service agreements, provide quotes or scheduling follow-ups, and coordinate requested towing or roadside services.
  • Communicate about service changes, access questions, vehicle information, and relevant follow-up.
  • Review job applications, assess qualifications, and contact applicants or references where appropriate.
  • Understand site traffic, maintain site performance, improve content, and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Keep records needed for legitimate business purposes, safety, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

We ask that you avoid adding highly sensitive information to general message boxes unless necessary for your request.

When Information Is Shared

We may share information with personnel or service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website or handle a request. The current site uses Formspree to process submitted forms and Google Analytics for site analytics; web-hosting, network security, and infrastructure providers may also receive technical data.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to coordinate services you requested, to comply with lawful requests or legal duties, to protect safety or rights, or in connection with a business transaction as permitted by law.

We do not sell personal information submitted through our website forms or use that information for unrelated third-party advertising. If these practices change, this policy will be updated and any additional notices or permissions required by law will be provided.

Cookies & Analytics Choices

Google Analytics and supporting website services may use cookies or similar identifiers. These technologies help us understand website use, monitor performance, and maintain security.

You can use your browser settings to control cookies, though disabling some storage or scripts may affect website features. Google also provides an Analytics opt-out browser add-on. We will update this information if we introduce additional tracking technologies or choices that require separate notice.

Data Retention & Security

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including responding to requests, reviewing applications, resolving disputes, and meeting legal or recordkeeping obligations. The actual retention period depends on the type of information and applicable requirements rather than a single fixed time period for every form.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information involved and rely on our service providers for parts of the website’s technical infrastructure. No internet system or form submission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Access, Correction & Deletion Requests

You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct inaccurate details, or to request deletion when appropriate. We may need to verify your identity before responding and may retain records when required or permitted by law.

Depending on the law that applies to Tow Time and your circumstances, you may have additional rights relating to your personal information. You can submit a request by emailing towtimellc@outlook.com with the subject “Privacy Request” or by calling (512) 364-8781. Please do not send sensitive identifiers by ordinary email.

Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not designed for children under 13, and we do not intentionally solicit their personal information through website forms. If you believe a child submitted personal information, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

External Websites

Links to social media accounts, map services, business partners, and other outside websites are provided for convenience. Their data practices are governed by their own policies. This policy does not cover websites operated by others.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, information practices, or legal requirements change. Any revised policy will be posted here with an updated effective date. Material changes may require additional notice or consent where applicable.
