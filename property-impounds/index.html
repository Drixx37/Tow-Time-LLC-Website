TEXAS COMPLIANCE NOTE

Signs are the beginning—not the entire legal test.

Texas law generally requires properly placed tow-away signs to have been posted for at least 24 hours before a sign-based private-property tow. Other authorization, notice, and facility requirements may also apply. We evaluate those requirements for the proposed operation before beginning.

See the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation signage guidance ↗.

A website inquiry does not establish towing authorization; a separate written agreement and lawful process are required.

QUESTIONS PROPERTY MANAGERS ASK

THE DETAILS, UP FRONT.

How much does it cost to get started?

We currently charge $30 per custom tow-away sign. A property with three applicable entrances would have an estimated entrance-sign cost of $90. The required number and locations of signs, plus any additional contractual terms, are reviewed before you agree to proceed.

How many signs does my property need?

Typically, you'll need signage at each entrance where vehicles can enter, but the required number can vary with the property layout and applicable signage rules. We'll review the entrances and any other necessary locations before ordering signs.

How soon can towing begin?

We generally estimate 3–7 days after the agreement is signed for the custom-sign process. Even when signs are ready sooner, sign-based towing cannot begin until compliant signs have been posted for at least 24 hours and all other legal and operational conditions are satisfied.

Who decides which vehicles can be towed?

The parking-facility owner or properly authorized representative establishes parking rules and authorization under a written arrangement. Any actual removal must comply with the applicable law; submitting the website form does not authorize a tow.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the Austin area and nearby Central Texas communities, including Cedar Park, Round Rock, Pflugerville, and surrounding areas. Whether your specific property is within the active service area will be confirmed during your inquiry.
